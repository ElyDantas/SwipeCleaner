Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy describes how SwipeCleaner, an Android app developed by Ely Dantas Andrade, and its informational website handle data. Contact us at app.swipe.cleaner@gmail.com.

Data accessed on your device

With your permission, SwipeCleaner reads photos and videos to display previews, calculate storage use, find similar media, and let you choose items to delete. The Files feature also reads folders you explicitly select. These features may process file names, paths, sizes, dates, and media content on your device. The Apps feature reads installed-app information; if you enable Usage Access in Android settings, it also reads usage statistics to sort apps by frequency. You can change permissions in Android settings and remove access to selected folders.

Similarity analysis and file organization run on the device. We do not intentionally upload your photos, videos, file contents, file paths, or installed-app list to our servers. Files are deleted only after you choose a deletion action and any required Android confirmation. Deleting a file does not remove copies held in backups or other services.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your optional display name, settings, selected-folder permissions, scan summaries, and cleanup history locally to provide its features. No account is required. You can clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove this local information, although Android backup or device-transfer settings may retain or restore a copy.

Ads, analytics, and diagnostics

SwipeCleaner is supported by Google AdMob ads. Google may process advertising identifiers, device and network information, and ad interactions to deliver, measure, and protect ads. The app also uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand screen visits, feature use, scan and cleanup counts, and permission outcomes. Our custom analytics events use feature categories and counts rather than your name, media content, file paths, or installed-app names.

Firebase Crashlytics receives crash and error diagnostics. Firebase Performance Monitoring may collect app performance, network, device, and installation information. These services can process technical data such as device identifiers, operating-system and app versions, IP-derived approximate location, timestamps, and diagnostic messages according to their own policies. The app does not request precise location permission. Visiting our website may also cause Firebase Hosting to process request information, including IP address.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy, AdMob privacy information, and Firebase privacy information. Links you choose to open, such as Google Play, Instagram, or the feedback form, are handled by those services under their own policies.

Retention and your choices

Local app data remains until you clear app data or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup settings. Google services retain data according to their applicable policies and settings. Uninstalling SwipeCleaner stops future collection by the app but does not automatically erase data already processed by third-party services. You can revoke media, notification, and Usage Access permissions in Android settings. For a privacy inquiry or a request concerning data within our control, email app.swipe.cleaner@gmail.com.

Children and security

SwipeCleaner is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us. We use Android's permission controls and device-local processing for the cleaning features, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version and its effective date will be posted here and in the app. You can reach us at app.swipe.cleaner@gmail.com.
